ABOUT MY APPROACH

Start with the problem. Design for the result.

My work begins with understanding what the learning problem is, what people need to do differently, which potential roadblocks may stand in their way, and determining which type of learning or performance support will make the biggest difference.

I translate complex processes, systems, theory, and requirements into intuitive learner-centered experiences through instructional strategy, multimedia development, storytelling, and emerging learning technologies.
