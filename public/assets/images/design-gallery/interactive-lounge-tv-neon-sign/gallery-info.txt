SHORT CARD DESCRIPTION:
Interactive and immersive lounge area to promoting company benefits with working and dimmable custom neon sign, buttons, and a realistic fly-out TV.

FULL DESCRIPTION:
This interaction was created for the purpose of introducing a specific company benefit and I wanted it and it's supporting resources to be available all on one slide. This lounge slide serves the purpose of a main menu with branching navigation. You can click the neon sign to dim it off or fade back on. The TV flys out towards your face as you click it to start the animated video.
